## Summary
Evaluated the architectural feasibility and trade-offs of handling unique email constraints at the application layer versus the database layer, and successfully resolved the type casting mismatch (`PostgrestException 22P02`) when migrating production-fidelity data from Firebase to Supabase.

1. **Trade-off Analysis**: 
   - Application-layer validation introduces potential race conditions in high-concurrency environments (Time-of-Check to Time-of-Use).
   - Database-layer constraints (such as a unique index) offer absolute data integrity guarantees but are slightly more rigid.
   - Decided that a hybrid approach—enforcing case-insensitive unique constraints at the PostgreSQL database level while intercepting database-level unique violations (error `23505`) at the repository/controller layer to present friendly error UI—is the optimal long-term strategy.

   - Standardized all relational foreign keys (e.g. mapping `customerId` on deposits back to the customer's UUID) to use this conversion layer, fully preserving referential integrity across the entire graph.
