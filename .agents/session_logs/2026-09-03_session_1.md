# Session Log: 2026-09-03 (Session 1)

## Goal
Implement Phase 1 of the Recurring Deposit (RD) Monthly Ledger & Transaction Tracking feature, and refine the architectural division of labor between the Flutter app and PostgreSQL database.

## Architectural Refinements ("Brain & Muscle" Split)
We critically analyzed the design of the RD ledger feature and aligned on a robust, scalable separation of concerns:
*   **The Flutter App (Dart) is the Brain**: Houses 100% of the active business mathematics, date offsets (1–15th vs 16–end), 1% late fee calculations, and chronological payment allocation cascade logic. This enables microsecond-fast, offline-capable unit testing, avoids duplicated codebases, and provides real-time, interactive payment preview screens for agents.
*   **The Database (PostgreSQL) is the Muscle**: Enforces database integrity constraints (`ON DELETE CASCADE`, unique indexes, and text check constraints), limits RLS access to authenticated owner agents, and processes writes atomically in a single network roundtrip using specialized RPC procedures.
