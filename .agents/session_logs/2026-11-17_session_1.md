# Session Log — 2026-11-17 (Session 1)

## Objective
The owner asked whether the paddings, margins and gaps are right across the app, said some places looked off (the dashboard or login, and the Monthly Hub), and asked for them to be fixed. The fixes go on `feat/m3-expressive-ui` (PR #20), committed at logical steps and pushed; `chore/dev-experience`, which is stacked on this branch, has to be rebuilt on it afterwards.

## How it was checked
- Every screen was rendered in widget tests with the real font, at 360x800 and, new this session, through the router on a 412x915 phone with system bars (36dp status bar, 24dp gesture bar) and the navigation bar. The earlier renders had no system bars and no navigation bar, and 360dp is narrower than most phones; the scheme strip's fault only shows at 412dp.
- A measuring test printed the rectangles of the blocks of each screen (app bar, search pill, groups, section titles, fields, tiles, buttons), so that margins and gaps could be compared as numbers across screens rather than by eye.
- Still not run on a phone, an emulator or in a browser.

## 1. Monthly Hub
- **Status stripe.** The colour bar on a row's leading edge started 12dp from the top and bottom with rounded ends, flush with the card's edge. The card's 20dp corners cut those ends at an angle, so it read as a strip stuck onto the card. It is now a stripe down the full height of the row, cut cleanly by the row's own corners (`EntityListTile.indicatorColor`; the one-time deposit list uses it too).
- **Status pill height.** In a row with a button the pill sat 8dp lower than in a row without one: the button brings 4dp of touch padding above and below, and the pill is centred on it. Both kinds of row are in every month. `EntityListTile.footerSpacing` lets the Hub drop the 8dp above the status line when it holds a button; the pill is now within 2dp in both, and a row with a button is 8dp shorter.
- **Rhythm.** The blocks above the rows were 8, 12, 12 and 12dp apart; all are 12dp (month switcher, tiles, search pill, due window, rows). The tiles keep 8dp between themselves.
- **Tile text** is inset 16dp, as a row's content is, so "To collect" starts on the edge the avatars and pills start on. It was 12dp.
- **Toolbar toggles** are the Material icon button: 40dp to look at in a 48dp target. Their fill was 44dp with no padding, so two toggles that were on touched each other.
- **Search hint.** "Serial, account or name" was cut to "Serial, account or …" at 360dp. The Hub's pill now uses the rule the other lists use (`searchHintThatFits`, moved out of `AppSearchBar`): the hint when it fits, "Search..." when it does not.
- **Batch bar.** The Deposit button is 12dp from the bar's end, as it is from its top and bottom; it was 8dp, off-centre in the pill's rounded end.

Tests: `rd_hub_view_test.dart` gains a "Monthly Hub spacing" group: the pill's height in rows with and without a button (fails at 10dp without the fix), the 12dp steps, the tile text edge, the hint at a narrow and a roomy width, the gap between two toggles that are on, and the batch bar's button. `list_rows_test.dart` checks the stripe's rectangle and that the row's surface clips it, for each position in a group. No existing test was changed; `pumpHub` gained a `width` argument.

## 2. Dashboard
- **Scheme strip.** On a 412dp phone the third pill ended 2dp from the edge of the screen: it looked cut off by the edge and gave no sign that a fourth follows. When every pill fits, the pills now share the width between the margins. When they do not, the strip scrolls and each pill is as wide as leaves half of the next one showing (2½ pills at 360dp and at 412dp). The pill's width is measured from its figure, so a count with more digits is not squeezed.
- **Chart card.** The rows of controls were 16dp and then 8dp apart (title row, scheme filter, "Include RDs"); they are 12dp apart. The chart keeps a 38dp band above its tallest bar for that bar's tooltip, and a further 16dp sat on top of it; that is now 4dp, so 12dp less is empty between the controls and the bars.
- **Tall tile.** The Customers tile pins its badge to one corner and its figure to another. It was inset 16dp at the sides and 12dp at top and bottom; it is 16dp on every side. Its height does not change (the two short tiles beside it set it).
- Looked at and left: the chart's own insets (28dp each side of labels and plot, symmetric), the login screen (centred, 24 / 8 / 64 / 8dp between its parts).

Tests: `test/features/dashboard_spacing_test.dart` (new) checks, at 360dp and 412dp, that the last pill on screen is cut by about half and that no pill ends within 8dp of the edge; that on a wide window all pills are on screen, equally wide, between the margins; that the chart's control rows are evenly spaced; and that the tall tile's badge is as far from its top as from its side.
