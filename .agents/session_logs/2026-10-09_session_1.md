# Session Log: 2026-10-09 (Session 1)

## Objective
1. Investigate why Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP) showed different time periods between the Tenure field and the top highlight banner on `OneTimeDepositDetailScreen`, and why KVP maturity dates were 1 month ahead of the Post Office passbook (e.g., at `7.6%`, showing `9 Years, 6 Months` / `114` months instead of `9 Years, 5 Months` / `113` months).
2. Implement an official Post Office investment-date-to-rate/tenure schedule (`PostOfficeRateSchedule`) across all schemes (`KVP`, `NSC`, `MIS`, `TD` 1/2/3/5Y, `RD`) with auto-fill from `startDate` and editable rate/tenure fallback.

## Root Cause Findings
1. **1-Decimal-Place Rate Truncation + `.round()` Overshoot**:
   - To double in exactly **113 months** (`9Y 5M`), the true compound annual rate is $(2^{12/113} - 1) \times 100 = 7.6385\%$, which the Ministry of Finance notifies rounded to 1 decimal place as **`7.6%`**.
   - Plugging truncated `7.6%` into `12 * ln(2) / ln(1.076)` yields `113.5524` months, and `.round()` rounded it up to **`114` months (`9Y 6M`)** (+1 month ahead of the passbook). The same +1 month overshoot occurred at `6.9%` (`124.66` -> `125` instead of `124` months) and `7.8%` (`110.74` -> `111` instead of `110` months).
2. **Detail Screen Dual-Source Mismatch**:
   - `OneTimeDepositDetailScreen` rendered `deposit.projection.note` in `KvpMultiplierBanner` (computed dynamically from `interestRate` without `termMonths`) while the `DetailItem` below it displayed stored `deposit.termYears` and `deposit.termMonths` directly from the database.

## Key Changes Implemented
- **`lib/core/models/postal_scheme_terms.dart` & `lib/core/models/investment_projection.dart`**:
  - Created `@freezed sealed class PostalSchemeTerms` (`interestRate`, `termYears`, `termMonths`, `totalMonths`).
  - Extended `InvestmentProjection` with `interestRate`, `termYears`, and `termMonths` so the UI projection card displays the resolved postal terms directly.
- **`lib/core/services/postal_rate_service.dart`**:
  - Created Riverpod-injectable `PostalRateService` (`postalRateServiceProvider`) using Dart 3 Records (`_CircularRatePeriod`) for official Ministry of Finance / Department of Posts historical circulars (2000–Present) across `KVP`, `NSC`, `MIS`, `TD` (`1Y`, `2Y`, `3Y`, `5Y`), and `RD`.
  - Implemented `resolveOneTimeSchemeTerms`, `resolveRecurringSchemeTerms`, and `calculateKvpTermMonths` to return official notified tenures at creation/form time.
- **`lib/core/services/projection_calculator.dart`**:
  - Made `interestRate`, `termYears`, and `termMonths` optional across `calculateRD`, `calculateTD`, `calculateMIS`, `calculateNSC`, `calculateKVP`, and `calculateOneTimeDeposit`, resolving official terms from `PostalRateService` via `startDate` when omitted and strictly honoring explicit `termYears`/`termMonths` when provided.
- **Domain Models (`OneTimeDeposit` & `RecurringDeposit`) & Controllers (Contractual Snapshot Pattern)**:
  - Updated `OneTimeDeposit.create`, `RecurringDeposit.create`, `OneTimeDepositsController.saveOneTimeDeposit`, and `RecurringDepositsController.saveRecurringDeposit` so `startDate` resolves defaults via `PostalRateService` while honoring optional rate/tenure overrides.
  - On read, `OneTimeDeposit` and `RecurringDeposit` strictly trust the stored DB snapshot (`interestRate`, `termYears`, `termMonths`) without calling `PostalRateService` on read.
- **Presentation Forms, Hooks & Detail Screens**:
  - In `OneTimeDepositFormScreen` (`useOneTimeDepositForm`) and `RecurringDepositFormScreen` (`useRecurringDepositForm`), `Interest Rate (%)` and `AppDurationInput` (`Term (Years)` / `Term (Months)`) are automatically populated from `postalRateServiceProvider` whenever `startDate`, `schemeType`, or TD tenure changes, while remaining editable in the UI for newly announced circulars or edge-case overrides.
  - Preserved `AsyncSingleEntityBuilder` (`oneTimeDepositByIdProvider` / `recurringDepositByIdProvider`) on `main` across detail and form screens.
- **`lib/core/mocks/fake_data_source.dart` & `test/post_office_rate_schedule_and_kvp_test.dart`**:
  - Updated `FakeDataSource` to resolve rates from `PostalRateService` based on `startDate`.
  - Added 7 unit tests covering `postalRateServiceProvider` DI, start-date-only `OneTimeDeposit.create` and `RecurringDeposit.create` across all schemes, boundary KVP rates, contractual snapshot fidelity on read, and custom overrides.
