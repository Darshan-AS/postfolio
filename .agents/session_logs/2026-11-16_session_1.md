# Session Log — 2026-11-16 (Session 1)

## Objective
Act on the design critique written after the Material 3 Expressive redesign (branch `feat/m3-expressive-ui`). The owner asked for the changes to be made and committed at logical steps. Scope taken: the items of the critique that are layout only. Items that need new Slang strings, provider or hook changes, or a product decision are listed at the end and not done.

Presentation only: no controller, provider, domain, data, routing or i18n file is changed, and no Slang key is added.

## 1. Monthly Hub density
The Hub is the screen an agent works in all month. On an 800dp phone it showed one account on entry, and each account took about 230dp.

- **Header tiles** are two lines: the label with the number of accounts, then the figure. Four tiles take about 136dp; they took 216dp. The caption and the cash / UPI split are still read out with the tile but no longer drawn; the split is in the visit planner. The count is an icon and a number, because "125 accounts" does not fit beside "Receivable" in a 160dp tile.
- **Group headers** are drawn only for a customer with several accounts. A header over a single account repeated the name the row already carried and offered "Select All" for one row. Rows under a header are titled by serial and account instead of the name.
- **The row shows its figure once.** "owes ₹15,600" sat above a full-width "Collect ₹15,600". The action now sits at the end of the status pill's line and carries the figure; a Deposit button, which has none, has the figure in the caption line; a row without an action shows the figure where the action would be. A row with a caption is about 146dp; it was about 176dp plus a 55dp header.
- On entry an 800dp phone now shows the first two accounts and part of a third.
- Not done: moving the month into the app bar. The app bar already holds the title, the theme switch and the view switch; the month fits only once the view switch moves out (see 3).

Tests: `rd_hub_view_test.dart` gives one customer a second account and checks that only that customer has a header and that its rows are titled by account, that a row shows the amount to collect once (on its button), that the pill and the action share a line, and that the four tiles take under 150dp at 360dp. Two existing cases in that file were updated for the new structure ("Select All" exists once; the arrears state is recognised by its action, not by the removed "owes" figure).

## 2. Red means overdue
Red was marking three things: what is overdue, the main action, and the agent's own money. In a month with arrears every row was red from top to bottom.

- **Hub actions**: Collect is the filled brand-colour button whether or not the account is overdue. It turned solid red, which reads as a destructive action. Deposit is a tonal button; it took the status colours of its row. The caption under the action is neutral.
- **Sheets**: the "from your pocket" line on the deposit sheet and the two "from pocket" lines on the visit sheet use the fronted colour (`AppFinancialColors.fronted.onContainer`), as the Fronted pill and the Receivable tile do. They were the error red.
- Red stays on the overdue and arrears pills, the indicator bar, the "5d late" text, the To collect and Arrears tiles when something is overdue, and the owed line of the detail header.

Tests: `rd_hub_view_test.dart` checks that Collect is filled with no colour override in a month where every account is in arrears, and that Deposit is tonal. `rd_sheet_colours_test.dart` (new) checks, in light and dark, that the deposit sheet draws a line in the fronted colour and none in the error colour when the agent is fronting.

## 3. The Accounts / Monthly Hub switch says what it is
The switch was a folder icon and a calendar icon among the app bar's actions. It is the way into the Hub, the app's main workflow.

- It is now two labelled segments ("Accounts", "Monthly Hub") in the app bar's title slot. The title it replaces, "Recurring Deposits", is what the navigation bar already says; a screen reader still gets it as the name of the control. No height is taken from the list.
- `ShellAppBar` gains `titleControl` for this. Its labels stop growing at 1.3x text, like the navigation bar's, and the control shrinks rather than push the theme button out.
- This is instead of moving the month into the app bar, listed under 1: the bar is no longer spending its width on a repeated title.

Tests: `rd_view_switch_test.dart` (new) checks that both views are named in the app bar, that a tap switches the view both ways, and that at 1.0x, 1.3x and 2.0x text the switch stays inside the bar and clear of the theme button.

## 4. The detail header is one row
A detail screen opened with a 120dp avatar, a centred headline, the supporting lines and a badge, each on its own line: about 330dp before anything the screen is opened for. On a recurring deposit the tabs started past the middle of an 800dp phone.

- **`EntityDetailHeader`** is a row: a 64dp avatar (`AppDimensions.avatarLg`), the name in `titleLarge` with the supporting line under it, and the badge at the end of the row. A new `footer` slot takes a full-width block under the row; actions follow from the start edge. Nothing is centred any more.
- **Above 1.3x text** only the name stays beside the avatar. The supporting line and the badge go under the row at full width; beside the avatar a phone number broke in two.
- **Recurring deposit**: the progress bar and its two lines of figures are the `footer`, laid out from the start edge. The customer's name is still a button to the customer. `AppButton.text` gains `flushStart`, which pulls the button back by its own inset so its icon starts under the account number; the button keeps its size and touch target. The tabs now start at about 257dp; they started at about 450dp.
- **Customer**: the phone number and the email each stay on one line and shrink when they have to. An email was broken mid-word at large text.
- **One-time deposit**: the scheme name is a supporting line (`bodyMedium`), not a second title.
- Kept: the scheme name on the recurring deposit header. `test/widget_test.dart` expects it there once, and it matches the one-time deposit header.

Tests: `test/core/detail_header_test.dart` (new) checks the row (name and supporting line beside the avatar, badge at the row's end, block and actions under it from the start edge), the stacked layout above 1.3x and the row layout at exactly 1.3x; that a `flushStart` text button's icon starts where the text above it starts, left to right and right to left, and still takes a tap; that a recurring deposit's tabs start above the middle of a 360x800 phone, under a full-width progress bar; that its customer button starts on the account number's edge; and that at 1.0x and 2.0x text a customer's phone number and email are each one line inside the screen. No existing test was changed.

## 5. List rows carry less
- **Customers**: Call stays on the row; WhatsApp moves to the row's menu, above Edit and Delete. Two inline buttons left the text about 100dp at 360dp and "+91 98765 43201" broke in two. The customer's screen still has both as buttons.
- **Deposits**: a row shows a status badge only when the status is not Active. The lists open filtered to Active, so the badge was on every row. A deposit close to maturity or past it is still marked by the row's bar and the line under its title. The trailing block is two lines instead of three.
- **Search hints**: `AppSearchBar` shows the hint it is given when that fits the bar on one line, and "Search..." otherwise. "Search by accou…" was cut on all three lists on a phone; a wide window still says what can be searched for. The bar works the room out from its own width, the theme's insets, the leading icon and 48dp per trailing icon button.
- **Deposits on a customer's screen** are titled by account number with no second line. The title was the scheme, which the avatar already names, and "Monthly Income Sch…" was cut. `OneTimeDepositCard` gains `overrideSubtitle`, as the recurring card has.

Tests: `test/features/list_rows_test.dart` (new) checks that Call is on the row and WhatsApp is in the menu and both still reach the intent service; that the row has two buttons and the text column more than 40% of the row; that an active deposit has no badge in either list and a closed one says "Closed"; that the deposit rows of a customer's screen show the account number and neither the scheme nor the customer's name; and, sweeping the bar from 320dp to 2200dp in 6dp steps at 1.0x, 1.3x and 2.0x text, that the long hint is shown only where it is not cut. That sweep caught the first version of the estimate counting a trailing button as 44dp. No existing test was changed.

## 6. Forms: one way to save, labels read in full
- **One way to save.** A form had a tick in the app bar and a "Confirm" button at the end of about 1,600dp of fields. It now has one "Confirm" button under the fields (`FormScreenBody`), on screen without scrolling. It is part of the body, not the scaffold's bottom slot, so it stays above the keyboard. `FormAppBar` is back to a title and a back button.
- **Labels above 1.3x text.** A label inside a field is one line and was cut: "Select Cust…", "Installment Amo…", "Installments depo…", "Opening recei…" at 2.0x. Above 1.3x, `AppLabeledField` puts the label on a line of its own above the field, where it wraps; up to 1.3x nothing changes. `AppTextField`, `AppDropdownField` and the customer picker use it. The one-field inputs on the ledger sheets are not converted; their labels are short.
- **Fields of several rows** (Home Address, Notes) have the label and the icon on the first row. They were centred in the field. The decorator centres a prefix icon, so the icon is given padding underneath as tall as the rows after the first (`restingLines`); in a field that has grown past its resting height it sits that much below the first row.
- **The customer picker** wraps a long name instead of cutting it.
- Tried and left: dropping the outline of a field at rest. Without the line, a floating label straddles the top edge of the fill with nothing to sit on. Doing it properly means the filled-field layout with the label inside the container, which changes the height of every field, including the amount fields on the sheets.

Tests: `test/core/form_layout_test.dart` (new) checks that the customer form has one "Confirm", none in the app bar, on screen under the scrolling fields; that it saves (an empty form reports the required name); that with a 300dp keyboard it is above the keyboard and still saves; that it is inside a 360dp window at 2.0x and as wide as the form on a 1400dp window; that while saving it says so and ignores taps; that a label is inside its field up to 1.3x and above it, uncut, with the required marker, at 2.0x; and that a three-row field has its icon and label level with the first row.

One existing test was edited: `test/features/forms_and_hooks_test.dart` tapped the app bar tick by its tooltip (`find.byTooltip(t.common.confirm)`); it now taps the button by its label (`find.text(t.common.confirm)`). What it checks is unchanged: saving surfaces the CIF, Aadhaar and PAN errors.

## 7. A shape names a scheme
An avatar's shape meant four things. On deposit lists it was the scheme. On Hub rows it was the status, in eight shapes, three of them the shapes of other schemes. On payments it was cash, UPI, cheque or transfer. On customers it varied from person to person and meant nothing.

- On rows and headers a shape other than a circle now names a deposit scheme, and nothing else.
- **Customers** are all `AppAvatarShape.person` (a circle): the list, the picker and the customer's screen. `AppAvatarShape.forSeed` is removed.
- **Hub rows** have the recurring scheme's shape, as on the Accounts list. The status is the avatar's colour and the pill.
- **Payments** are `AppAvatarShape.neutral` (a circle). The mode is the icon inside.
- Unchanged: illustrations (empty states, the login badge, the dashboard's hero tile), which are not rows.

Tests: `test/core/avatar_shapes_test.dart` (new) checks that every scheme has a shape of its own and none is the person's or the neutral one; that every customer has the person's shape in the list and on their screen; that Hub rows have the recurring scheme's shape in a settled month and in a month with arrears; and that payment avatars are the neutral shape. No existing test was changed.

## 8. Closing a deposit is in the menu, with its label
The deposit screens had a bare seal icon in the app bar that closes (or reopens) the deposit. It is now an entry of the overflow menu, "Close" or "Reopen" with the same icon, above Delete, as on the deposit's row in the list. `EntityDetailScaffold.customActions` is replaced by `menuActions`. The app bar is left with Edit and the menu.

"Close" is the existing string. In a menu "Close deposit" would be clearer; that is a new string.

Tests: `test/features/deposit_detail_actions_test.dart` (new) checks, for a recurring and a one-time deposit, that nothing in the app bar is named "Close", that the menu has the entry above Delete, and that choosing it asks for confirmation.

## 9. Receipts are grouped by year
On the Transactions tab every month had a header. A deposit is paid once a month, so that was a header per receipt, above a receipt that already says "Paid on 13 Oct 2026". Receipts are now one run of grouped tiles per year, newest first. A receipt takes about 104dp; with its header it took about 146dp.

The pills naming the months a receipt was applied to ("#19 ₹400") are neutral. In the primary tone they looked like something to tap.

Tests: `deposit_detail_actions_test.dart` checks that there is one tile per receipt, one header per year and none per month, that the receipts run from the latest to the earliest, and that the pills are drawn in the neutral colour.
