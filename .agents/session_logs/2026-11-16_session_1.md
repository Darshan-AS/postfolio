## 1. Monthly Hub density
The Hub is the screen an agent works in all month. On an 800dp phone it showed one account on entry, and each account took about 230dp.

- **Header tiles** are two lines: the label with the number of accounts, then the figure. Four tiles take about 136dp; they took 216dp. The caption and the cash / UPI split are still read out with the tile but no longer drawn; the split is in the visit planner. The count is an icon and a number, because "125 accounts" does not fit beside "Receivable" in a 160dp tile.
- **Group headers** are drawn only for a customer with several accounts. A header over a single account repeated the name the row already carried and offered "Select All" for one row. Rows under a header are titled by serial and account instead of the name.
- **The row shows its figure once.** "owes ₹15,600" sat above a full-width "Collect ₹15,600". The action now sits at the end of the status pill's line and carries the figure; a Deposit button, which has none, has the figure in the caption line; a row without an action shows the figure where the action would be. A row with a caption is about 146dp; it was about 176dp plus a 55dp header.
- On entry an 800dp phone now shows the first two accounts and part of a third.
- Not done: moving the month into the app bar. The app bar already holds the title, the theme switch and the view switch; the month fits only once the view switch moves out (see 3).

Tests: `rd_hub_view_test.dart` gives one customer a second account and checks that only that customer has a header and that its rows are titled by account, that a row shows the amount to collect once (on its button), that the pill and the action share a line, and that the four tiles take under 150dp at 360dp. Two existing cases in that file were updated for the new structure ("Select All" exists once; the arrears state is recognised by its action, not by the removed "owes" figure).
