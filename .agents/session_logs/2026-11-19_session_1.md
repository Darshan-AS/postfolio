# Session log: 2026-11-19, session 1

## Objective
The owner asked whether the blog post "Start building with Material 3 Expressive" (m3.material.io, 13 May 2025) had been read, whether the app follows it, and whether the widgets in it are missing from the package or only from the app. Answered; nothing built.

## How it was read
The site is drawn by JavaScript, so a plain fetch gives an empty page. A browser agent opened the post, took the text from the page and described each figure from screenshots. The agent that writes this saw the text and one screenshot (the top of the page), not the figures. Before this the guidance had been read through search summaries only.

## The post's 14 components against the app
- **In the app, from the package**: button groups, buttons, extended FAB, FAB menu, loading indicator, navigation bar, navigation rail, progress indicators (wavy).
- **In the package, rendered on 2026-11-17 and held** (reasons in that day's log, part 3): app bar (overflows at 2.0x with a subtitle), split button (no tap action for a screen reader), toolbar (overflows at 2.0x). The app uses the official app bar and its own batch bar.
- **In the package, no job in the app**: sliders, the plain FAB (every list has an extended one).
- **In the package, never rendered here**: icon buttons. Recorded as a candidate under M18.
- None of the 14 is missing from the package.

## The post's seven tactics against the app
- Followed: shapes (avatars, empty states, sign-in), colour (vibrant scheme, status colours with a meaning), containment (grouped tiles on every list and detail screen), motion (springs, shared elements, morphing indicator).
- Partly: typography (emphasized styles throughout, no display-size figure anywhere), component flexibility (a rail exists; wide layouts are deferred), hero moments (the "month settled" moment is approved and not built; the dashboard's headline card is not decided).

## Examples for the owner (rendered, then taken out)
The owner asked to see examples before deciding. Both were put into the real screens with throw-away code, rendered at 360dp at 1.0x and 2.0x text, and restored; `git status` was clean afterwards.
- **The package's icon button** on the Hub's arrows and toggles and on a detail screen's Edit. In stills the gain is two outlines (a 48 by 56dp arrow, a 52 by 40dp Edit); a toggle that is on looks like today's. For a screen reader it is two nodes: the outer has the name and no tap action, the inner has the tap action and no name, and a plain button is reported as "not selected". A wrapper could give one node with both; that wrapper is not built. The agent's advice: leave the official icon button.
- **A month card at the top of the dashboard**: the month, accounts settled of accounts due as the wavy bar, one figure in the display size (money to collect, else cash in hand, else "All settled" with a shape and a tick), and a button into the Hub. It holds at 2.0x text. In the render the card's figure did not match the Hub's for the same month; the agent thinks the month's ledger had not loaded on the dashboard and has not confirmed it. A real card needs that settled first. The agent's advice: build it; the owner has to say which figure leads, the colour, the wording and where the button goes.
- **Owner**: the icon buttons stay the official ones; the month card is postponed.
- **Owner, on the comparison of 2026-11-18**: button groups stay everywhere. No tabs, no switch, no filter chips for the Hub's date ranges.

## The rest of M18, built
The owner asked for everything that was approved and not built. Each package widget was first rendered at 360dp, at 1.0x and 2.0x text, with the screen reader's tree printed; each new test was checked by breaking the code it covers, one change at a time.

### Built
- **A list's add button shrinks on scroll** (`lib/core/widgets/expressive/app_fab.dart`, the three list screens). The package's controller listens to the list. Two things are ours: a changed search or filter brings the label back (an empty result has nothing to scroll it back with), and a device set to remove animations keeps the label. Found on the way: the community button told a screen reader "New, New Customer", and "New" once the label was hidden. The wrapper now gives the label once.
- **Selection flip on Hub rows** (`app_selection_flip.dart`, `rd_monthly_operation_card.dart`). Avatar, then an empty mark in the scheme's shape, then a tick. The checkbox is gone from the tree; a `Semantics` node stands for it. The package's flip always takes 220ms, so the wrapper gives it no time on a device that removes animations.
- **Navigation badge** (`app_navigation.dart`, `main_shell_scaffold.dart`). `globalAgentFloatProvider` already counts accounts in arrears from the summary cache, so the shell loads no ledger. The package's badge is silent inside a destination; the destination's own label says "Recurring, N accounts in arrears".
- **The month's progress and its statement** (`rd_hub_month_progress.dart`). First built with the wavy bar and a statement for every settled month; three things changed after it was rendered and run:
  - the wavy bar animates for ever (`_spinController.repeat()` whenever the bar is wavy), which made a router test time out and would keep the Hub repainting: the flat track is used;
  - a settled past month says nothing, or every past month would carry the statement;
  - the block is keyed by the month and absent while the month loads, so that switching from an unfinished month to a settled one does not play the arrival. The key is pinned by a test. The "absent while loading" half is not: the fakes answer within a frame, so no test reaches it.
  - "Settled" here is the month's own installments. Money in hand for later months can still be waiting, so the statement says "Every installment due this month is collected and deposited".
- **Sign-in shape** (`app_shape_avatar.dart`, `login_screen.dart`). `MaterialShapeBorder` blends two shapes in `lerpFrom` / `lerpTo`; a `TweenAnimationBuilder` over a `ShapeBorderTween` is all that is ours. It waits for the content's fade, then turns for the spatial duration, on the effects curve so that it never asks for a shape past the second one.
