## Key Findings Summary
- **P0 Security & Data-Loss Defects Identified**:
  1. Internal `SECURITY DEFINER` helpers (`replace_account_nominees`, `upsert_account_identity`, `get_account_nominees`) in `public` are exposed via PostgREST RPC to `authenticated` without `auth.uid()` checks.
  2. Toggling deposit status from OTD/RD list cards feeds partial `_listColumns` entities (`nominees: []`, `openingReceivable: 0`) into full-replacement `save_*` RPCs, silently deleting all nominees and wiping `opening_receivable` (or throwing `check_violation` on locked RDs).
  3. `RDLedgerController._mutate` appends `tx` / `event` onto post-commit `_readFacts(rdId)` without deduplicating by `id`, writing 2x double-counted values to `rd_ledger_summary` when collecting from the Monthly Hub; `save_recurring_deposit` unconditionally deletes `rd_ledger_summary` on metadata edits; `undoBatch` refreshes all 766 RDs instead of affected `rd_id`s.
  4. `pubspec.yaml` bundles `assets: - data/` (including local PII migration logs) into client builds, and `web/index.html` loads an unpinned synchronous third-party script (`corbado/flutter-passkeys`).
- **Structural Redesign Blueprint**: Documented across 8 sections + a 5-phase roadmap (Phase 0 Hotfixes -> Phase 4 MPKBY/SAS Business Suite) in conversation artifact `postfolio_redesign_analysis.md`.
