## Full Description
Postfolio is a fast, modern portfolio and monthly ledger companion built for Post Office savings agents to organize customer accounts, track Recurring Deposit (RD) collections, and project maturity payouts across postal savings schemes.

KEY FEATURES
• Customer Portfolio Directory: Keep customer details, nominee allocations, savings accounts, and linked deposits organized in one searchable place.
• Recurring Deposit (RD) Monthly Hub: See every account due this month at a glance — track what is settled, collected in hand, overdue, or paid from pocket — and record Post Office deposits cleanly.
• RD Passbook & Ledger: Record customer payments, track Post Office deposits and default fees, and inspect month-by-month installment history.
• One-Time Deposits & Projections: Track Time Deposit (TD), Monthly Income Scheme (MIS), National Savings Certificate (NSC), and Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP) accounts with automatic maturity and payout projections.
• Portfolio Dashboard: View active holdings, scheme-wise breakdowns, and upcoming maturities in one clean overview.
• Instant Demo Mode: Explore every screen with realistic sample data before signing in — nothing in Demo Mode is sent to the cloud.
• Accessible & Modern Design: Built with Material 3, Light/Dark/High-Contrast themes, and large-text support.

PRIVACY & SECURITY
Your data is isolated to your authenticated account with Row-Level Security and encrypted in transit. Postfolio contains no ads and does not sell or share your data.

DISCLAIMER
Postfolio is an independent record-keeping and calculation tool for Post Office agents. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to India Post or the Government of India.
